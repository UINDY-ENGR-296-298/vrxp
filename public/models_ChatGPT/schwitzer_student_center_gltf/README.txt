This is an approximate, reference-based glTF proxy of the Schwitzer Student Center at UIndy,
built from the provided photos. It includes the main front rotunda, left colonnade/glass wing,
right finned hall massing, the chapel/logo block, rear amphitheater massing, site walks/roads,
and simple landscape/site elements.
